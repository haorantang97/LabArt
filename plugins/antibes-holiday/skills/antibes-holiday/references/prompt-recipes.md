# Prompt Recipes

Use these as scaffolds. Do not name a source identity or request an exact style match.

## Reference Role

When reference crops are supplied, prepend:

```text
The reference images are evidence only for physical pen behavior, tempo, shorthand,
open contours, and stopping discipline. Ignore and do not reproduce their people,
accessories, poses, compositions, or recurring motifs. Apply the line behavior to
the new subject below.
```

For scene references, use this stricter version:

```text
Use the references only to study causal staging, unequal scale, information-density
hierarchy, active blank space, edge pressure, and the contrast between long event
paths and compact narrative hinges. Do not reproduce their actors, accessories,
props, poses, scale ratios, hinge positions, or layouts. Choose a different
composition family and build the new event independently.
```

When only the bundled calibration sheet is supplied, prepend:

```text
The reference is an original non-semantic pen-stroke calibration sheet. Ignore
all swatch shapes, positions, and composition. Do not turn them into objects,
symbols, borders, or decorations. Use only the physical line behavior: quick
committed sweeps, slight pressure and speed changes, shallow kinks, selective
retracing, local ink darkening, dry or blunt terminals, and restrained paper
tooth. Construct the requested subject independently.
```

## Relaxed Illustration

```text
Create an original quick black-pen drawing of [subject] [action or state].
It should look drawn in one alert sitting with a real fine black pen on plain
uncoated warm-white paper, not rendered as clean digital line art.

Begin with one or two fast structural sweeps. Preserve slight changes in pressure,
curve tension, and speed; allow an abrupt terminal and one shallow kink. Add a
compact knot of short shorthand marks only at [action/contact/recognition point].
Let one selected overlap or retrace become darker because the pen slowed down.
Leave recoverable boundaries unfinished. Allow no more than two incomplete search
marks when they support the main movement. Stop before the subject is fully
described, corrected, centered, or polished.

Keep subtle authentic ink deposition and paper tooth at close range, but no heavy
paper texture, camera shadow, pencil shading, wash, brush effect, sketch filter,
uniform wobble, vector-perfect curves, decorative distress, signature, or text.
Do not import subjects or motifs from the references.
```

## Non-Human Living Form

```text
Draw [animal/plant/organism] through center of gravity, movement, and one
distinctive anatomical rhythm. Use one fast contour for mass plus direction and
three-to-six shorthand fragments around [behavior/contact]. Omit most anatomy and
stop while the viewer can still complete the form. No human facial expression,
clothing, accessories, hands, mascot posture, or cartoon traits.
```

## Functional Object

```text
Draw [object] through how it operates rather than its complete casing. Use one
quick structural sweep and preserve only [opening/joint/contact/feed path/control].
Concentrate short darker marks at the operational knot. Let an incoming or outgoing
material path perform a second compositional job. Do not close the product outline,
correct it into industrial-design geometry, or add a face, labels, arrows,
technical-diagram clutter, sparkles, or stock icon conventions.
```

## Abstract Relationship

```text
Choose an observable physical proxy for [relationship], such as [cord/ring/load/
opening/branch/fold/barrier/route/transfer], then draw that event rather than a
diagram. Give each force a different tempo, tension, interval, or closure pattern.
Let them interact at one compact darkened knot and release into [resulting
movement/state]. Use no
people, faces, text, arrows, boxes, funnel, flowchart, lightbulb, brain, puzzle,
rocket, handshake, heart, sparkle, or familiar idea icon. The result must retain
fast sweeps, shorthand fragments, and an unforced stopping point.
```

## Story Scene

```text
Create one original sparse black-pen story scene showing [event]. The viewer should
infer [cause] and [consequence] from one frame without captions.

Assign unequal narrative roles: [principal actor], [counterforce], and one compact
[narrative hinge]. Use only two or three event beats. Connect them through one
observable causal path such as a reach, shared baseline, gaze, object trajectory,
pour, cable, fold, gap, or continuous contour, but no arrows or motion icons.

Break conventional balance with exactly one main device: [oversized operational
feature / dominant cropped mass / tiny displaced counteractor / off-center hinge /
edge-reaching contour]. Let scale follow narrative importance rather than realistic
perspective. Give the actors unequal detail density. Keep recognition marks compact
at the hinge and let blank paper carry distance, pause, anticipation, or release.

Do not complete every body, prop, or setting. No symmetrical tableau, centered
triangle, equal-size actors, repeated accessories, scenic filling, background
architecture, perspective grid, caption, or decorative marks. Preserve fast
committed sweeps, local retracing, open contours, and independent terminals. Stop
when what just happened or will happen next is inferable.
```

## Disproportionate Portrait Or Object

```text
Draw [subject] with [feature or operational part] carrying the attitude or action.
Enlarge that feature enough to disturb the expected silhouette, center, or crop,
while keeping all other description sparse. Place the recognition knot beside or
beneath the dominant mass rather than centering it. Use one long contour to absorb
the imbalance and leave the opposite field quiet. The enlarged feature must be
required by the new concept, not borrowed from a reference. Do not exaggerate every
part, restore realistic proportions, or rebalance the margins.
```

## Logo Exploration

```text
Create one original relaxed black-pen gesture for [brand/concept]. Fuse [noun]
with [action/state/relationship]. Treat this as an expressive master, not a final
app icon. Use one committed sweep, one recognition knot, and at most three detached
fragments. Preserve pressure variation, a small kink, and open terminals. No face,
mascot, text, border, mockup, gradient, shadow, sparkle, checkmark, arrow, thick
rounded icon styling, or vector-perfect symmetry.
```

## Vector Handoff

```text
Reconstruct the selected gesture as an authored vector. Preserve its tempo,
asymmetry, terminal behavior, and recognition knot. Remove camera texture and
accidental pixel noise only. Do not normalize every curve, equalize every stroke,
close expressive gaps, or make the mark globally thicker. Create a display master
first, then an optically adjusted small-size production master if needed.
```

## Diagnostics

If it looks like clean vector minimalism:

- specify real fine black pen on uncoated paper;
- demand one-pass sweeps with pressure and speed changes;
- add one local retrace and one abrupt terminal;
- remove perfect curves, constant-width paths, and geometric centering.

If it looks generically sketchy:

- remove uniform wobble and decorative roughness;
- keep only one or two search marks;
- strengthen the fast-sweep versus slow-knot contrast;
- delete crosshatching and texture outside the recognition knot.

If it is too complete:

- remove half of the descriptive lines;
- open the largest silhouette;
- preserve only action, mass, and the recognition knot;
- stop before symmetry and proportion correction.

If it is too abstract:

- add one subject-specific shorthand fragment;
- strengthen the operational or anatomical rhythm;
- do not restore the full outline.

If it defaults to people:

- restate the non-human subject class;
- remove eyes, smiles, hands, clothing, and character posture;
- use anatomy, operation, material path, or spatial force as recognition.

If a scene looks staged or generic:

- identify one narrative hinge and delete unrelated setting;
- enlarge only the story-bearing actor, feature, or consequence;
- reduce supporting actors to posture plus one recognition fragment;
- move the densest knot away from the geometric center;
- let a causal sweep or active gap replace balanced grouping;
- remove arrows, speed marks, and equal-detail rendering.
