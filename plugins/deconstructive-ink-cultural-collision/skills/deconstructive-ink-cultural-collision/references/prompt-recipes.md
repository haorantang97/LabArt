## Candidate gate

Generate two independent candidates for each new subject family. Compare them against assets/calibration/user-accepted-boneless-anatomy.png without feeding that image to the generator.

Pass only if both candidates have:

- a recognizable dominant subject;
- mostly value-built rather than outline-built form;
- semi-wet, narrowly feathered ink edges;
- layered gray-to-black darks;
- dense focal detail and generous surrounding paper white.

Reject concept-art rendering, pen-first drawing, dry hatching, repeated microtexture, random splash, watercolor blooms, or low-information broad washes.

## General-purpose routing template

First classify the subject by its dominant problem: organic form, soft material, force-bearing machinery, structural space, compact artifact, actor-machine contact, crowded circulation, or dynamic environment. Use only the matching modifier with the core boneless ink recipe.
