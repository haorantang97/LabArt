2 · Key point

The record is the maker. The brief holds sixty photographs on grey card and sixty entries of 120 words; nothing else exists. Opposite: the hand that was never written down.

3 · Readymade (from the brief)

The photographer's grey card and the museum's object record: accession number, date, place, technique, dimensions, how acquired. Nothing imported.

4 · Object

190 × 240 mm, the card's proportion; 128 pp = 8 × 16; one grey uncoated 120 gsm, self-cover; section-sewn; CMYK on grey for the plates, black for text; 1,200 copies, 378 g each.

0–1 · Position, questions

The curator decides; "a catalogue" is the convention, "makers never recorded" is the task. It goes to the second city alone, then to the next unrecorded group as a record format.

★
Strange decision

Sixty colour photographs of ceramics printed on grey uncoated paper, so that the photographer's card is the page and each vessel stands on it as it stood in the studio. Convention is coated white for glaze; we do not, because the record is the maker and the grey card is the only ground all sixty share.

Strike it and what changes

The stock, the cover, the plate rule and the proof all change: on white the card edge would have to be cut out, the plates bordered, and the proof would answer a different question. It is not severable. Proof, week zero: the lightest and darkest glaze on the grey sheet.
