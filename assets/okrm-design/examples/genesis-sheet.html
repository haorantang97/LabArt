Position

The curator decides and has already written and photographed everything. The brief says "a catalogue"; the task is to give 60 unrecorded makers the standing named artists get by default. We ask to be present when the second venue is confirmed.

1
Three questions

What: a record of work by people history did not record. Why now: the show lasts nine months in two cities; the book is what outlives it. Where: into collections where the makers' absence should still be felt. Context: catalogues assume an author to celebrate.

2
Key point

Intuition: 60 entries of 120 words by unknown makers are already a register, like an inventory of unclaimed property. Reason: the museum's accession ledger already treats the vessels this way. Opposite: the photographs, which make each vessel a portrait.

3
Framework

Stage: one run, one merchant, no foil, two cities. Actors: 60 vessels, 60 entries, 60 photographs, 2 venues. Script: the book is an accession ledger; portraits are tipped in as plates. It pretends to be the ledger, ruled lines and the column "maker: unknown" included.

4
Object and economy

170 × 280 mm, sewn, cloth spine strip, no jacket. Register on ledger-coloured uncoated stock; plates on a smoother stock from the same mill, interleaved every four pages. No foil → blind deboss. Two cities → a second ruled column, empty, stamped by hand at the second venue.

5
Method

Curator authors the register; we edit the sequence; the photographer is credited as a maker. Accession numbers 001–060 replace page numbers. Fixed: grid, register voice, headings. Variable: plate stock, venue stamp. Brief: write "maker" as the form would, not as prose.

6
Making

One day of sequencing with prints on a table, grouped by what the ledger cannot say: hand, scale, colour. One change of pace: a spread of the grey card with no vessel.

7
Production

Keep: two stocks, sewn sections, the empty column. Give up: cloth colour, deboss depth, plate size. The proof answers one question: does the ledger stock hold fine rules without bleeding.

8
Continuation

Second printing at the second venue with the column stamped. The register becomes the museum's template for future shows of unrecorded makers.

★

Strange decision. Accession numbers replace page numbers and the second venue's column is printed empty, to be stamped by hand nine months later. The convention is a finished book. We do not finish it, because the key point is that the record is incomplete and the show is still travelling.
